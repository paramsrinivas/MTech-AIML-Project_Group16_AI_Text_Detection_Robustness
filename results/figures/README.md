# Results Figures

This directory contains figures generated from the AI Text Detection Robustness experiments.

## Figures

- `baseline_metrics.png` — baseline classification metrics.
- `metrics_comparison.png` — comparison of baseline metrics.
- `confusion_matrices.png` — baseline confusion matrices.
- `final_confusion_matrices.png` — final evaluation confusion matrices.
- `final_metrics_comparison.png` — comparison across original, surface-edited, and semantically paraphrased AI text.
- `ai_detection_rate_before_after_edits.png` — AI detection before and after text edits.
- `robustness_condition_metrics.png` — human false-positive rate and AI recall under the evaluated conditions.
- `source_recall.png` — model recall by data source.

The figures should be interpreted alongside the CSV metrics and the final report. Results describe this experiment and dataset; they do not establish performance for every AI detector or writing style.
